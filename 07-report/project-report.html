Customer Voice Hub

An AI assistant and automation setup that helps an after-sales quality team at a car maker act on customer complaints faster: it reads every complaint, links repeated problems together, and tells the right team what to do first. Built for Hyundai Motor India as the example company, using made-up demo data.

Built by Subha Das, MBA Business Analytics, Chandigarh UniversityBuilt 19–20 Oct 2026For Hyundai Motor India Management Trainee interview
1 · Summary

What was built, in one paragraph

Customer complaints arrive from reviews, social media and service centres. The same fault often shows up as many separate complaints, so nobody sees the bigger problem until it becomes serious. Customer Voice Hub fixes that. Two automation tools score each complaint and rank open service cases by risk. An AI assistant (Claude) uses those tools, follows written company rules, and drafts the follow-up emails and meetings. Everything is shown on one website, and a chatbot lets anyone ask questions in plain English or Hinglish.

2 · The problem

Why this matters

  • Repeated faults hide in plain sight. In the demo data, a gearbox jerking problem (called DCT judder) appears in 16 separate tickets across the Venue, Verna and Creta in 8 cities. Each service advisor sees only one car.
  • Urgent cases wait in the queue. Some tickets are safety-related or on their third workshop visit, but they look the same as routine cases.
  • Customers write in mixed languages. Complaints like "Exter ka AC bilkul thanda nahi karta" are easy for people to understand but are missed by simple keyword checks.
  • Reporting takes time. Someone has to pull numbers, write the escalation email and set up the review meeting by hand.
3 · The solution

How a complaint moves through the system

Step 1A customer writes a review, post or complaint
Step 2Tool 1 translates it if needed and scores it: tone, topic, urgency, owner team
Step 3Tool 2 checks open tickets: risk score 0–100 and repeated faults
Step 4The AI assistant applies company rules and explains its reasoning
Step 5Action: email draft, meeting, leadership summary or customer reply

The parts

Tool 1 · Review IntelligenceTranslates Hindi or Hinglish to English with Google's AI, then scores the complaint with fixed rules: positive or negative, 12 topics (gearbox, AC, sunroof and so on), urgency, safety and churn warnings, and the team that should actn8n workflow + Google Gemini
Tool 2 · Escalation RadarReads all service tickets, gives each open ticket a 0–100 risk score from five rules (days open, repeat visits, safety, repeated fault, fleet customer), and groups tickets about the same faultn8n workflow

ChatbotA separate chatbot anyone can open in a browser; it uses the same two tools and answers in short, formatted repliesn8n AI Agent + Gemini

6 · Problems solved along the way

What went wrong and how it was fixed

ProblemCauseFix
n8n page would not openn8n had been stopped by mistakeStarted it again; learned the start and stop commands
"Bad gateway" on the public addressThe public address could not find n8n inside DockerPut both on one private Docker network
Public address changed on every restartThe first free option gives a random address each timeMoved to a free ngrok account with a permanent address
ngrok rejected the keyThe domain ID was pasted instead of the keyStored the real key separately and checked its length
"Model no longer available"Google retired the Gemini model n8n pickedSwitched to the current Gemini Flash model
Translation took 60 secondsThe model was "thinking" before every answerChose a lighter model and capped the answer length: now about 1 second
Chatbot showed "Error in workflow"Google's server was busy (error 503) on a preview modelMoved to a stable model, added automatic retries and a backup model
If Google's AI fails during a callOutside services can go downThe translation step continues without AI, so the tool still gives a rule-based answer
7 · Limitations

What to be clear about

  • Synthetic data. All tickets and reviews are made up to look realistic. The numbers show the method, not Hyundai's real performance.
  • Live only during demos. The tools and chatbot run on the laptop, so they are offline when it is switched off.
  • Keyword-based scoring. Gemini translates Hindi and Hinglish, but the scoring rules can still miss sarcasm or new phrasing.
